User Data Deletion Instructions

Application: qfx · Last updated: November 9, 2026

You can ask us to delete the data the qfx application holds about you at any time. Follow either option below.

Option 1: Remove the App from Instagram

  1. Open Instagram and go to Settings → Apps and websites (or Website permissions).
  2. Find qfx in the list of connected apps.
  3. Select Remove. This revokes the App's access to your account.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

  1. Send an email to qifani35@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request - qfx".
  2. Include your Instagram username so we can locate your data.
  3. We will confirm receipt and delete your data within 30 days, then notify you when it is complete.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your Instagram account ID and username stored by the App.
  • Access tokens issued for your account.
  • Stored comments, @mentions, and related media and comment IDs associated with your account.
  • Logs that contain your identifiers, where technically feasible.

Information we are legally required to retain (for example, for security or legal compliance) may be kept for the legally required period and will not be used for any other purpose.
