Privacy Policy

Application: qfx · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [YOUR_NAME_OR_COMPANY] ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you connect an Instagram account to the qfx application (the "App"). The App is a social media monitoring tool that detects @mentions and comments on Instagram Professional (Business or Creator) accounts.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Instagram Professional account and grant permissions, the App may receive:

  • Account information: Instagram account ID and username.
  • Content and interaction data: comments on your media, @mentions of your account in comments and captions, and related media IDs and comment IDs.
  • Authentication data: access tokens issued by Meta so the App can act on your behalf within the permissions you granted.
  • Technical data: webhook event metadata (timestamps, event type) and server logs used for debugging.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or private messages unless you explicitly grant the corresponding permission.

2. How We Use Information

  • To detect and display @mentions and comments related to your account.
  • To provide monitoring, reporting, and notification features inside the App.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling unrelated to the App's features.

2a. Permissions We Request

The App requests only the Instagram permissions necessary to detect and read comments and @mentions on your account (for example, instagram_basic and instagram_manage_comments, or their Instagram Login equivalents). Each permission is used solely for the feature described above.

3. Sharing of Information

We share information only with: (a) Meta Platforms, as required to operate the Instagram API; (b) infrastructure providers that host the App, under confidentiality obligations; and (c) authorities when required by law. We do not share your data with other third parties for their own purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed to provide the App's features or as required by law. Access tokens are removed when you disconnect your account. You may request deletion at any time (see section 6).

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to stored credentials. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can disconnect the App at any time from your Instagram settings (Settings → Apps and websites) and request deletion of all data we hold about you. Instructions are available on our Data Deletion page, or you can email qifani35@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for individuals under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes to This Policy
